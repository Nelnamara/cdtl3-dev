CDTL3 Looks: Getting Started

Pick one look per idea, or none, and add a note.

Starter setups by role

Quick Style gains whole setups for Starter, Healer, Tank and DPS that decide which lanes you have, what goes on each and the time limits that suit the role.

One clear estimate-versus-exact marking

Every icon and bar running on CDTL3's own estimate instead of the game's timer gets one look, a tooltip line says which it is, and the lane settings explain the mark in one line. Estimates happen on Retail and Forever, in combat.

A first-run welcome that explains the screen

On a first install a short welcome says what the lanes are, with Show me, Lock in place and Open settings buttons, and What's new moves behind its own button.

Plain names and a Simple settings view

A Simple view holds the few settings most people change, and jargon settings get plain names, a one-line example and a sentence at the top of each tab.

Minimap tooltip and a Help tab

The minimap tooltip says what each click does, and a Help tab answers the common questions with exact steps and shows the /cdtl3 auras report in a window instead of chat.

Save and share your own looks

Save the current look under your own name, share it as a string, and apply any look to one lane, bar frame or ready frame, or to all of them.
