CDTL3 Looks: The Original's Effects

The original CooldownTimeline's looks, rebuilt in our own code. Every drawing below moves the way the effect does in the game: timings and sizes come from the original's own values. Pick one per idea, or none, and add a note. Not a look choice, so not here: Glow and Flash Icon will show without a highlight border texture (GitHub issue #13's bug).

Highlight effects to offer

For the spells you mark Highlight in Filters, in Lanes › Icons › Highlight Style (and the Ready frames'). V2 kept four and dropped the original's Pulse and Bounce; the original also had Shake and Scale in its code but hidden from its settings. Each choice is the list the dropdown would hold, every effect drawn moving.

When a cooldown reaches the Ready end

TeZwo's screenshot (GitHub issues #13 and #14, from the original) catches a big, see-through copy of an icon at the lane's Ready end, mid-way through growing and fading out as the cooldown finished. CDTL3 has nothing there today. Each drawing loops: the icon slides to Ready, then finishes.

Lanes, Ready frames and bar frames showing and hiding

When a frame shows or hides (out of combat, or nothing cooling down). The original let you set each frame's animation: Fade or Scale, how long, from and to, how many loops, and bounce; bar frames faded over a second. V2 replaced all of it with one fixed 0.3 s fade and no settings.

Five splits

Split Count goes up to 5. Icons move evenly in time between neighbouring marks, as today and as in the original; what differs is where the marks sit by default. The copy of the original we read (v1.0) stopped at 3 and spaced marks evenly; TeZwo's screenshot is from a later original with five, where the marks crowd towards the long end.
