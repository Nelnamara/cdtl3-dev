Raid cooldown roster

Shows other raid members' big cooldowns, such as Rallying Cry, Divine Hymn and Revival, one per caster, read from the combat log, with a tick list in Filters to pick which spells count.

#18 · MoP Classic, TBC Anniversary and Classic Era only. Retail and Forever close the combat log to addons, so it can't be built there. Nothing like it exists today, so there is no Today choice.

Externals I gave: target and time left

After you cast an external such as Ironbark, CDTL3 also shows the effect on your target, with their name and the time it has left, not only your own cooldown.

#19 · Exact on Classic Era, TBC Anniversary and MoP Classic (from the combat log). On Retail and Forever the effect time is an estimate from your cast, in dimmed text, and it can't see the buff being removed early.

Burst pack: lust, Sated, trinkets, potion

One Burst pack button under Filters › Defaults adds timers for lust, Sated, your two trinkets (with the proc cooldowns the built-in data lacks) and your potion, and sends them all to one burst place.

#24 · Exact on MoP Classic and TBC Anniversary. TBC has Bloodlust and Heroism but no Sated debuff; Classic Era has neither, so it gets trinkets and potion only. Retail and Forever: partly, since buffs that land in combat show late or as estimates and item timers are estimates. Shown here: a MoP Hunter mid-lust.

Fight clock and cooldown forecast

A fight clock with marks at 2:00, 3:00 and 5:00 of the fight, showing where each pinned cooldown will be at those marks, even past the lane's Max Time.

#27 · Exact on Classic Era, TBC Anniversary and MoP Classic. On Retail and Forever, icons still slide from the game's own timer, but anything that works out "back by 3:00" is an estimate from your cast. Shown here: a Fire Mage 1:12 into a fight.

A what's-ready board for defensives

A ready frame mode, Stay until used, that keeps every chosen defensive on show: ready ones bright, cooling ones dimmed with their timer.

#28 · Every client. Whether a spell is ready stays readable in combat on Retail and Forever too. Putting the cooling ones in order of time left is the one part Retail and Forever can't do in combat. Shown here: a Protection Warrior.
