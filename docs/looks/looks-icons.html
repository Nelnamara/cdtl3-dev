CDTL3 look board

Icons, bars and markers

Pick one look per idea, or none, and add a note.

Charge count and recharge on the icon

Shows how many charges a spell has left on its icon while the next charge recharges along the lane, and goes to Ready when one comes back.

fp02Partly on Retail and Forever: the count can be shown in combat but not compared, so nothing can react to a number of charges there, and charges in combat are so far proven only by the test harness. Today: a stacks tag; the spell charge code is commented out.

Alerts when a buff lands or drops

Per buff, plays a sound and flashes when the buff is gained (procs, externals) or when it ends (active mitigation).

fp05Full on Classic Era, TBC and MoP; on Retail and Forever, in combat only for buffs in the Cooldown Manager's Tracked Bars. Today: no gained or fell-off alert.

Mana bar beside the mana cooldowns

Shows a healer's mana, with a percent, next to the lane that holds the mana cooldowns such as Innervate, Mana Tea and Hymn of Hope.

fp17Possible on every client; the percent text only partly on Retail and Forever, where in combat the bar fills but the percent can't be read. Today: lane tracking has Class Power, hard to find.

Resource bar with a threshold colour

Tracks a tank's rage, Holy Power or Brewmaster stagger on the lane and changes colour at a number the player sets.

fp31Possible on Classic; on Retail and Forever the hidden value can fill the bar but not be compared, so a colour that depends on the number stays fixed in combat. Today: Health, Class Power and Combo Points tracking.

Burst window marker

Draws a line on the lane at a chosen "in N s" point and lights up the pinned cooldowns that will be ready before it.

fp22Possible on Classic; partly on Retail and Forever, where the line draws but which icons are ready before it can't be worked out in combat. Drawn on a Linear (Time) lane, where each second sits at one spot. Today: Pinned and Highlight.

Ready-soon warning

Warns a set number of seconds before a chosen cooldown is ready, with an icon cue and an optional sound.

fp25Possible on Classic; partly on Retail and Forever, where while the time is hidden the cue can only come from an estimate. Today: Dynamic Color recolours bars only.

Pandemic refresh window on DoTs

Per debuff, sets a refresh window as a percent of its length and recolours the icon or bar inside it, with one cue.

fp23Classic Era, TBC and MoP only; blocked on Retail and Forever, where addons can't see debuffs on enemies. Today: Dynamic Color does a time-based warning, on bars only.

GCD-aware icons

Dims icons whose only wait is the global cooldown, and tints the GCD marker when the next ready icon lands inside it.

fp26Partly on Retail and Forever: the game always says when a spell is waiting only on the GCD, but whether a cooldown lands inside the GCD can't be worked out in combat. Today: a GCD lane tracking option.
